Currently, my research is grounded in the metacontrol theoretical framework, focusing on the cognitive and neural mechanisms of cognitive control, with a particular interest in how the dopamine system shapes individual differences in interference control and adaptive behavior. I combine behavioral experiments, EEG, and neuromelanin-sensitive MRI to investigate how stable neurobiological traits influence human cognitive performance.
